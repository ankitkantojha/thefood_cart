Our Story

Crafted with Passion

The Food Cart began with a simple belief — that great food has the power to create happiness. Nestled in the heart of Mithanpura, Muzaffarpur, we bring you an experience that is as warm as our kitchen.

From crispy street-style bites to indulgent fast food favourites, every dish is prepared fresh, with ingredients chosen for quality and flavour. Come hungry, leave happy — that's our promise.
